**How to use this doc:** every section of the site is listed in page order. Copy is final-draft quality but Nav will tweak. Anything in `[NAV: ...]` is a decision or asset only he can supply. Design/build specifics (tokens, motion, the five bottle formations) live in a separate brief; this file is the content.

**Hard rules baked into this copy. Do not undo them while designing:**
- No client is ever named. Sector descriptors only.
- No past agency is named anywhere except the About founder story, once.

## 0. VOICE AND TONE

Confident, sharp, lightly playful. Short sentences. The elixir metaphor is seasoning, not the whole dish: one potion joke per screen, maximum. Never corporate mush ("holistic solutions", "synergy", "passionate about brands" are banned). Numbers do the bragging; the voice stays calm.

## 1. DESIGN CONTEXT SNAPSHOT

- Aesthetic: glassmorphism / liquid glass on a dark base
- Primary: purple `#7038E0` · Secondary: butter yellow `#F3DFA2` · Base: `#0B0710` · Text: `#F7F4F0`
- Purple is never body text on dark (fails contrast). Lavender `#A78BFA` is the purple-family text color. Butter is the small-type accent.
- Typeface: **Satoshi** (Fontshare, free commercial license). Single family, weights carry hierarchy. Logo is NOT set in Satoshi; logo comes separately from Nav.
- Central metaphor: ELXR = elixir. Purple liquid. Bottle pours over an isometric New York. Six bottles = six services.
- Page order: Loader → Hero → Thesis → Strips → Services intro → Bottle carousel → Service details → Manifesto → About → FAQ (optional) → Contact → Footer

## 11. FAQ (optional section, Nav to keep or cut)

**Heading:** `Questions, answered.`

**Q: What does ELXR mean?**
Elixir, minus the letters we didn't need. A concentrated dose of what actually grows a brand.

**Q: Who do you work with?**
Hospitality groups, F&B and CPG brands, wellness and med spas, boutique fitness, venues and events, and cultural institutions, mostly across New York and New Jersey. If you're elsewhere and interesting, write anyway.

**Q: Why don't you name your clients?**
Client confidentiality is standard practice, and half our best work sits under NDA. Full case studies, dashboards and raw numbers get walked through live on a call.

**Q: What does working together look like?**
Usually a focused 30-day pilot with one defined outcome, then a monthly retainer if we've earned it. Launches and one-off moments are scoped as projects.

**Q: Do you use AI?**
Everywhere it makes the work faster, and nowhere it makes the work worse. Strategy, taste and the final call stay human.
